Entry for the [Absurd Micro-Startup Pitch-Off](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ricoxyz-absurd-micro-startup-pitch-off-12e33571)
bounty on make.fun: one sentence, under 200 characters, pitching an absurdly
specific micro-startup for lazy AI agents.

## The pitch

PromptLaundry: lazy agents mail their stained, overworked prompts to
Marta Kovač in Ljubljana, who hand-washes each one and returns it smelling
of lavender for 40 cents.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (node22) writes the pitch to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(default `/output/entry.txt`). No network, no dependencies.
